## Project Overview
SMARTNEST Store wants to analyze its 2026 sales data to understand customer purchasing behavior and identify opportunities to increase sales in 2027.

## Business Objective
Analyzed sales and customer data to identify:

- Sales trends
- Customer demographics
- Top-performing states
- Sales channels
- Product categories
- Order status patterns

##  Dataset

The dataset contains information about customer orders,
including order date, gender, age, state, channel,
category, amount and order status.

## Data Cleaning

The following steps were performed:

- Checked for missing values
- Checked for duplicate records
- Standardized data
- Verified data types
- Created age groups
- Extracted month from order date

## Business Questions

1. Which month had the highest sales?
2. Which gender contributed more to sales?
3. Which states generated the most sales?
4. Which channel generated the most sales?
5. Which category was the best performing?
6. What is the relationship between age and gender?
7. What were the different order statuses?

## Key Insights
Women drive the sales. They contributed 64% of total sales (13.56M of 21.18M), versus 36% from men.
Maharashtra is the top state. It has the highest sales at 2.99M, followed by Karnataka (2.65M) and Uttar Pradesh (2.10M).
Three channels dominate. Amazon (35%), Myntra (23%) and Flipkart (22%) together bring 80% of all orders.
Strong order fulfilment. 92% of orders were delivered. Only 8% were cancelled, returned or refunded.
November is the peak month. It has the highest sales and orders, while December is the weakest.
Adult women lead the segments. They place the most orders (about 35%), ahead of teenage women (21%) and adult men (15%).

## Recommendations for 2027
Target women, especially adults. Run focused campaigns and new collections for this segment.
Double down where it works. Prioritise Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart and the top 3 states, and test growth in other states.
Plan around the seasons. Stock up and launch offers before November, and add promotions for December.
